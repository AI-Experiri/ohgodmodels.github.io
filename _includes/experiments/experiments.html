Evidence that motivates us

  • Cramming (Geiping & Goldstein, 2022) asked how far a single GPU gets in one day. For BERT, the answer was quite far.
  • SmolLM2 already performs well at our target size: the 360M model scores 54.5% on HellaSwag.
  • L20-Edu-135M (2026) was built end to end on one NVIDIA L20 GPU in about 72 GPU-hours. It still trails SmolLM2-135M, and that is the gap we want to close.

Our commitments

  • Code, weights, and training logs are public.
  • Only public data, with every method and setting published.
  • Hypotheses are registered before the experiments run.
  • Negative results are published with the same rigor.
